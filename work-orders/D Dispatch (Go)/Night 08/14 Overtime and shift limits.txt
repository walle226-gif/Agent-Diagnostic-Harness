Overtime and shift limits

Now: Shifts can grow without limit.

Should: A workday is never longer than 10 hours. Time after 8 hours counts 1.5 times in the plan's cost, so the planner prefers spreading work over overtime.

Part 1: The 10-hour limit.
Done when: A test with too much work leaves jobs unassigned with the reason "shift limit".

Part 2: Overtime in the cost.
Done when: A test where work can be split between two technicians avoids overtime.

Every part's new test must fail on today's code and pass after the part.
