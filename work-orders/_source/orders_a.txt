=== A01 | 1 | feature | Voucher numbers per series
Now: Vouchers are saved without a number, so there is no way to refer to "voucher A12" or to see that none are missing.
Should: Every saved voucher gets the next number in its series (A manual, K customer invoices, L supplier invoices), with no gaps and no reuse.
P: Add a series and a number to the voucher model. Saving assigns the next free number in the series.
D: A test saves three A vouchers and one K voucher and gets A1, A2, A3 and K1.
P: "ledger list" shows the number first on each line, and "ledger add" takes "--series" (default A).
D: A CLI test adds a voucher with "--series K" and "ledger list" shows K1.
P: Numbers survive a restart: reloading the company file continues each series.
D: A test saves A1, reloads the company from disk, saves again and gets A2.
