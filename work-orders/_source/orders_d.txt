=== D01 | 1 | feature | Time windows

Should: A job starts inside its window [earliest, latest]. A technician who arrives early waits. A job that cannot fit anywhere is listed as unassigned with the reason "time window".
P: Checking windows in the planner, with waiting.
D: Tests cover an early arrival that waits and a job started on time.
P: Unassigned jobs with a reason.
D: A test with a job whose window has passed lists it with "time window".
