Large ledgers stay fast

Now: Nobody knows how the ledger behaves with a real year's worth of vouchers.

Should: With 100,000 vouchers, "ledger balance" finishes in under 5 seconds.

Part 1: "ledger generate --count N --seed S" creates realistic, balanced random vouchers for testing; the same seed gives the same data.
Done when: A test generates 1,000 vouchers twice with one seed and gets identical files, all balanced.

Part 2: Make loading and the trial balance fast enough.
Done when: A test with 100,000 generated vouchers runs "balance" in under 5 seconds.

Every part's new test must fail on today's code and pass after the part.
