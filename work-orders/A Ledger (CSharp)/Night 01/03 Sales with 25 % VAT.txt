Sales with 25 % VAT

Now: A sale has to be entered as a hand-made voucher with three lines, and the VAT worked out by hand.

Should: "ledger sale --gross 1249.00 --text ..." creates a K voucher: 1510 debit gross, 3001 credit net, 2610 credit VAT. VAT is gross × 0.20, rounded to whole öre, half away from zero, and net is gross minus VAT.

Part 1: A VAT split function in Core (gross to net and VAT at 25 %) with the rounding rule above.
Done when: Tests cover 1249.00, 99.99 and 0.05 SEK with exact expected amounts.

Part 2: The "ledger sale" command builds and saves the voucher.
Done when: A CLI test adds a sale, and "ledger balance" shows 1510, 2610 and 3001 with the right amounts.

Every part's new test must fail on today's code and pass after the part.
