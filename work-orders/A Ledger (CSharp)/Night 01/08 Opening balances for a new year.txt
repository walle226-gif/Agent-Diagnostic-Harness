Opening balances for a new year

Now: A new year has to be started by hand.

Should: "ledger open-year 2027" creates an opening voucher dated 2027-01-01 in series I. Balance accounts (1000–2999) carry their closing 2026 balance. Result accounts (3000–8999) are not carried; the year's result goes to 2099 so the voucher balances.

Part 1: The opening calculation and voucher in Core.
Done when: A test with a small 2026 year checks every line of the opening voucher.

Part 2: The CLI command, refused if 2027 already has an opening voucher.
Done when: A CLI test runs it twice; the second run fails with a clear message and nothing new is saved.

Every part's new test must fail on today's code and pass after the part.
